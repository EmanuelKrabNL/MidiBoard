## What does it do?
When you press the 'key looking things', while it is connected to a midi input supporting device, and the midi supporting device is actively running a midi supported application, and the firmware which makes the midi device input for the midi supporting device running the midi supported application is flashed, it should be ready to go!

## Why is this suited for my little sister?
I have put the letters of the notes on the midi device which is supported by the midi- okay ill stop... ;-; Okay, so, the letters give my little sister an indication of what note she is playing, and i have also decided to make a higher note actually higher! That way she knows how high or low a note will sound like without thinking!

## What can my midiboard do?
The left mechanical switch makes you go an octave lower, the right one makes you go an octave higher, and the middle one plays a random key!
The left slider 'does' the audio/volume, and the right one modulation!
Ive also added lights cause why not!
